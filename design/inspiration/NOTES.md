# What the references say

## UI (`ui/`)
- **Menus, buttons, HUD:** dark brown/black panels with ornate antique-gold frames, thorny/flame-like corner flourishes, a small round jewel at the bottom center. Engraved serif capitals (PLAY, MENU).
- **Quest log / the Book:** aged parchment scroll with rolled ends, wax seal, colored diamond bullets, thin progress bar.
- **HUD layout (from the bridge screenshot):** small health/heart emblem top-left, round parchment minimap top-right, third-person camera behind the character.

## World (`world/`)
- **Style:** hand-inked illustration. Fine linework, crosshatching, Escher-like libraries, a hand-drawn town map seen from above. Some pieces in full color (Moebius-like green forest, blue cathedral library with flying books, overgrown sunlit library), some black and white.
- **Surreal moments:** a boy sitting on a floor made of text, a figure raking up eyes, someone lying in a bed covered in sheep, hands drawn as fingerprint lines. These fit the demons well.
- **Places:** deep libraries with ladders, a tree made of books with hanging lanterns, a dense rooftop town around a plaza.
- Two images (unicorn skulls, the unicorn-and-tower crest) are motifs from the Murakami novel itself. Good for mood; the game's own versions should be original.

## Mood (`mood/`)
- Night: deep blue and purple sky, mist filling the valley, warm orange lantern light, a castle/tower far off on a hill.
- Warm amber light on wood (the bridge).

## Direction
- **3D world:** ink-and-paper look. Cel shading with dark outlines and a paper texture over everything, so the 3D town reads like one of the illustrations. Night palette blue/purple with orange lantern light.
- **UI:** dark panels with gold frames for menus and HUD; parchment for the Book and the quest log.
