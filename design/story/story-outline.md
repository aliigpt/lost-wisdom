## Premise

Every night the Reader goes into the clock tower to transcribe, and has to leave the clock tower by morning. On the way to the tower, the Reader is often struck by demons that try to stop them from completing their work.

Demons are very good at making us forget our bigger purpose in life, the things we are building and working on. So the Reader needs to fight them whenever they try to keep the Reader from the tower.

There are also surprise demons during the transcription itself.

### 1. The Fog (depression)

Stops the Reader from going into the clock tower. The fog floats in the night sky. The Reader has to avoid it by walking or running. If the Reader is struck by it, they are left on the ground, paralyzed and unable to move for a minute, even though the fog has already left.
